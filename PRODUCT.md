# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nana is for users who want an intimate simulated-phone role-play space with AI
characters. They return to the app in short, emotional moments: chatting,
calling, adjusting a character, reviewing memory, and later sharing music,
shopping objects, or offline story scenes.

## Product Purpose

Nana turns an AI relationship into a private phone. Chat is the entry point,
calls create intimacy, music carries emotion, shopping creates relationship
objects, offline text adventure creates shared experience, and memory turns all
of it into a long-term relationship.

## Brand Personality

Tender, private, and alive. The interface should feel dreamlike and romantic
without becoming childish, generic, or overloaded with decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid copying competitor feature volume directly. Do not add old-object or
secondhand-object rituals as a product lane. Avoid generic AI SaaS styling,
template pink stores, heavy glitter, water droplets, fake glass clutter, and
decorative screens whose data never returns to memory.

## Design Principles

- Relationship actions first: every surface should help the user talk, call,
  remember, repair, or prepare a shared experience.
- One material language: the phone shell uses a crystal-cloud system, with soft
  cloud controls emerging from a quiet crystal desktop.
- Ornament must earn its place: keep sparkle rare, keep text readable, and let
  interaction states carry the delight.
- Native capabilities stay behind service boundaries, while the UI can already
  show convincing states for voice, camera, and video.
- New app lanes should write toward one long-term relationship memory, not
  isolated fake apps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Prioritize readable contrast on small phones, stable touch targets near 44 px or
larger, reduced visual clutter, and motion that communicates state without
blocking repeated use.
